Types

Menu

A menu

A menu can have its items divided evenly

Secondary Menu

A menu can adjust its appearance to de-emphasize its contents

Pointing

A menu can point to show its relationship to nearby content

A secondary menu can also point

Tabular

A menu can be formatted to show tabs of information

A tabular menu can be attached to a segment

Text

A menu can be formatted for text content

Vertical Menu

A vertical menu displays elements vertically

Vertical menus can be secondary, pointing or text menus

Pagination

A pagination menu is specially formatted to present links to pages of content

Content

Header

A menu item may include a header or may itself be a header

Use suiHeader on an item, or suiMenuHeader inside an item, to add a header.

Text

A vertical menu item can include any type of text content

Input

A menu item can contain an input inside of it

Button

A menu item can contain a button inside of it

Link Item

A menu may contain a link item, or an item formatted as if it is a link

Dropdown Item

An item may contain a nested menu in a dropdown

Menu

A menu may contain another menu group in the same level as menu items

Use suiSubMenu with suiRight to float a group of items to the right of the menu.

States

Hover

A menu item can be hovered

Menu items are only hoverable if they are links, <a> or suiLink.

Active

A menu item can be active

Disabled

A menu item can be disabled

Variations

Stackable

A menu can stack at mobile resolutions

Inverted

A menu may have its colors inverted to show greater contrast

Colored

Additional colors can be specified

A whole menu can also be coloured, or coloured and inverted

Icons

A menu may have just icons

Labeled Icon

A menu may have labeled icons

Fluid

A vertical menu may take the size of its container

A horizontal menu will be fluid by default.

Compact

A menu can take up only the space necessary to fit its content

Evenly Divided

A menu can have its items divided evenly

Attached

A menu may be attached to other content segments

Size

A menu can vary in size

Fitted

A menu item or menu can remove element padding, vertically or horizontally

Borderless

A menu item or menu can have no borders
