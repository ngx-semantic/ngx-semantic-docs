Types

Shape

A shape can contain any content on each of its sides

Get a reference to the shape with #shape="suiShape" to call its flip methods.

Cube

A shape can be the size of a cube

Text

A shape can be formatted for text content

Content

Side

A shape displays one side at a time; you can choose the next side before flipping

setNextSide takes a CSS selector that is matched against each side.

Settings

Duration and Size

A shape can change its animation duration, and resize to fit the next side while animating

Methods

Method Description
flipUp() Flips the shape upward
flipDown() Flips the shape downward
flipLeft() Flips the shape to the left
flipRight() Flips the shape to the right
flipOver() Flips the shape over clock-wise
flipBack() Flips the shape back counter-clockwise
flip(behavior) Runs a named flip such as 'flip up'
setNextSide(selector) Sets the side to show on the next flip
isAnimating() Returns whether the shape is animating
reset() Removes all inline animation styles
repaint() Forces the browser to repaint the shape
refresh() Re-reads the sides, e.g. after changing them
